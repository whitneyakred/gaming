# HOLY SHIP! logo

`holy_ship_logo.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool, with
transparent background enabled. It is displayed with nearest texture filtering.

Prompt:

Create a polished transparent-background title logo for a cozy-but-dangerous top-down pixel-art sailing survival game. The exact and only text is HOLY SHIP! in large immediately readable chunky hand-pixelled cream parchment and muted golden wooden letters, dark brown pixel outlines and small shaded depth. Two balanced lines HOLY above SHIP!, or one compact centered composition. Integrate a small weathered Viking wooden sailing boat silhouette beneath the letters, a tiny cream sail, delicate rope accents and two small muted teal pixel waves, all part of one cohesive restrained emblem. No skulls, no extra words, no UI, no background, no border/frame around canvas. Strong readable lettering at 500px wide. Match low-resolution warm tan wood, dusty brown outlines, desaturated blue-green ocean and pale bone highlights of an indie 2D pixel game; deliberate square pixel clusters, no photorealism, no gradients, no smooth vector curves. Transparent padding modest, emblem centered. Warm adventurous and charming, premium indie-game title treatment.
