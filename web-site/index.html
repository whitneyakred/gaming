THE VOYAGE

Overview of the voyage

Bring a few friends, claim a spot on deck, and see where the sea takes you. Catch dinner, keep the ship afloat, and hop ashore when an island catches your eye. Somewhere out there are the keys to Paradise Island’s locked gate. Finding them is up to your crew.

Can you make it to Paradise?

What do I need to play?

A recent desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser with WebGL 2, a keyboard, and a mouse. Phones and touch controls aren’t supported yet.

Free early access gameplay

Jump in for free, solo or with up to three friends. There are no purchases or subscriptions. We’re still building the game, so expect a few bugs along the way. Online play may pause if our free hosting reaches capacity.

How does saving work?

Your sailor identity, preferences, and hosted voyage are saved on your device. The captain keeps the voyage save; clearing browser data removes it. The online service temporarily holds voyage names, crew membership, and connection identifiers. Cloudflare handles connection traffic. Please keep personal information out of voyage names.
