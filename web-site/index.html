ALL HANDS ON DECK

A little rough around the sails.

This is an early-access voyage. Expect unfinished islands and the occasional bug. Saves live on the captain’s device; clearing browser data removes local saves. Keep the host’s game tab in the foreground for smooth sailing.

What do I need to play?

A recent desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser with WebGL 2 and a keyboard and mouse. Phones and touch controls are not supported yet. Everyone should reload the page after a game update.

Is it really free?

Yes. This small community launch uses free hosting capacity. If the harbor reaches its daily capacity, online play may be temporarily unavailable. There are no purchases or subscriptions.

What gets stored?

Your sailor identity, preferences, and hosted voyage are stored on your device. The online service temporarily holds voyage names, crew membership, and connection identifiers. Private passwords are hashed, and the service uses temporary hashed network identifiers to limit abuse. Cloudflare handles the connection traffic. Don’t put personal information in voyage names.
